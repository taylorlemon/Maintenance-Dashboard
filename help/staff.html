StoneAnchor Quick Start
for maintenance techs and front desk

StoneAnchor is where the building's work orders, preventative maintenance (PM) and room turns live. Everything you add is saved with your name and the exact time, and nothing can ever be deleted — so it's also the record the state looks at.

1Signing in

  1. Go to my.stoneanchor.app.
  2. First time: open the invite email from StoneAnchor and click the link to set your password. Then you'll be asked your name once. It's shown on everything you add.
  3. After that: type your email and password and click Log In.
  4. Forgot your password? Type your email, click Forgot your password?, and use the link in the email.

2Put it on your phone's home screen

iPhone (Safari): open my.stoneanchor.app, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
Android (Chrome): open my.stoneanchor.app, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

3Add a work order

  1. Click the Room / location box at the top. A room is always required. It can be a number (214) or a place (OFFICE, LOBBY).
  2. Press Tab, type what's wrong, and press Enter.
  3. It's saved, and the cursor jumps back to Room so you can type the next one right away.
Tick Urgent before pressing Enter for anything that can't wait. Urgent items are at the top of the 6am email.

4Check it off

Click the round circle next to it. It's marked done with your name and the time, and drops off the list. Clicked the wrong one? Click Undo at the bottom of the screen within a few seconds.

Repeating PM tasks (like a weekly check) come straight back with their next due date when you check them off.

5Open one for notes, photos and details

  • Click a work order to open its panel. Click anywhere outside it to close it.
  • Add a note at the bottom: what you found, what you did, parts used. Notes are permanent and show your name and the time.
  • Photos: click the + under Photos. On a phone it offers the camera.
  • You can also change the room, due date, section, Urgent and how often it repeats.

6Made a mistake?

Nothing can be deleted. Open it and click Cancel task…, then say why (for example "Entered twice"). It leaves the list but stays on record.

7Find anything

Type in the search box: a room, a word, or something written in a note. Room history shows every request ever made for one room.

8List or Board

Board shows sections as columns. Drag a card to move it, for example from Requests to Active. Use the ⋯ next to a section name to add, rename or move sections.

9The 6am email

Every morning you'll get one email listing what's urgent, overdue or due today at your building. No email means nothing needs attention.
