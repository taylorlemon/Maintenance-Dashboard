A Reminder About Compliance Documentation
This tab is view-only — it shows how many compliance tasks are overdue or coming due for each community, pulled live from Asana. All compliance documentation (certificates, inspection reports, licenses, etc.) should be kept in ALIC — our official compliance software — not here. Each Executive Director and Office Manager is responsible for uploading their community's compliance documents into ALIC as needed.
Facilities
Add or remove the properties tracked in the system. A facility can be added without its Asana information — its Work Orders tab just stays empty until that's filled in. Removing a facility is permanent and only allowed once it has no projects, expenses, or to-dos on record.

Add a Facility
To find these IDs in Asana: open the facility's project, then use the "..." menu → Copy link. The long number at the end of that link is the Project ID. Open the Preventive Maintenance and Room Turn sections the same way to get their IDs. The Compliance Section ID instead comes from the shared "LPFM Compliance Tracking" board — open that facility's section there and copy its link the same way.
Manage User Access
To add a brand-new person: invite their email in Supabase (Dashboard → Authentication → Users → Invite user). They'll show up in the list below automatically — set their role and facilities here. Admin sees and manages everything, including this screen. Editor can add, edit, and delete within their assigned facilities only. Viewer can look but not change anything, within their assigned facilities only. Unchecking every facility cuts off their access without deleting their login; to fully delete a login, do that in the same Supabase screen.
